# Miracle Network R&D: New Prototype Concepts (Public)
Updated 2026-10-23. **Status: proposed / early prototype — not live, not audited, testnet only.**

Reality Connect / Miracle Network is exploring three new building blocks:
- **Proof of real-world activity** — an open, portable way for everyday devices to prove physical-world contributions, with independent checks instead of a single central server.
- **Fairer dual-coin usage** — an approach to paying for network services that rewards the people who power the network, without burning users' tokens.
- **Verifiable social rewards** — community rewards that independent parties can double-check, with built-in protection against fake engagement.

Early prototypes are in development and testing. Nothing is deployed, and no tokens, returns, or earnings are offered. More updates as work progresses.

Post-safe angle: "Building in the open: transparent, verifiable rewards for real-world and social contribution. Prototype stage — testnet only."
